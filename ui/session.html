On this page

Who's here

  • Tick each kid who came, or use Tick everyone, then click Save.
  • Only kids ticked as here count in the results. If a kid checks in but isn't ticked, their answers won't count until you tick them.
  • In a group without names, enter how many took part instead, so the report can show how many answered out of how many came.

Checking in

  • Kids scan the QR code with their phone's camera, or you can copy the link and send it to them.
  • For kids without a phone, Check in on this device lets them take turns on yours.
  • The check-ins list shows who hasn't checked in yet. Click Refresh to update it.
  • If a kid checks in twice, their new answers replace the old ones.

What the check-in asks

  • What this check-in asks lists this session's questions, when kids check in, and the activity goal.
  • For the questions about other kids, kids can tap Nobody. Kids can also skip any question. A skipped answer stays blank: it never counts as "nobody" or as a low score.
  • You can change the learning question until the first kid checks in. After that it's locked, so every kid answers the same question.

Results

Click See results for the numbers and the network picture, or Group report for a summary you can save, print or share with staff.
