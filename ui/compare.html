On this page

Choosing sessions

  • Pick a Before and an After session. The page starts with your first and latest sessions, and updates as soon as you pick others.
  • A single activity has only one check-in, so there's nothing to compare.

Who came and who answered

  • Shows how many kids attended and checked in each time, and how many checked in both times.
  • If different kids answered each time, an average can change even if nobody feels any different. A warning shows when that happens, and when the two sessions asked different questions or kids checked in at different times.

Who worked with or talked to whom

  • Only kids who came to both sessions are compared, so a change in who showed up doesn't look like a change in how kids got on.
  • Kids at both sessions: how many kids were here both times.
  • New connections: pairs of kids who connected in After but not in Before.
  • Lost connections: pairs who connected in Before but not in After.
  • Answered both times, no reported connections both times: kids who answered the "who" questions both times, but had no connections either time.

The pictures

  • Before and After use the same key as the results page. Green lines in After are new connections, and the kids in them get a green border.
  • In a group with group-only reports, names are hidden and the two pictures don't line up, so no kid can be picked out.

How kids felt

  • Each question is shown on its own, never added up into one score. Only questions asked both times are compared.
  • Everyone is the average of every kid who answered in each session. Answered both times only uses kids who answered in both sessions, which is the fairer comparison.

The warning

A yellow warning shows when the numbers point in different directions. For example, kids say they feel closer, but some kids had no reported connections both times. It's a sign to look at the whole picture, not just one number.
