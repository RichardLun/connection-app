On this page

Two kids count as connected if either one said they worked with or talked to the other today. Knowing each other before doesn't count. Only kids ticked as here are included.

The numbers

  • Kids here: how many kids were ticked as here.
  • Checked in: how many of them filled in the check-in.
  • Connections: how many pairs of kids connected. If Ana and Ben both pick each other, that's still one connection.
  • Share of possible pairs who connected: from 0 to 1. 1 would mean every kid connected with every other kid. 0.20 means 1 in every 5 possible pairs connected.
  • Kids with no connections: kids who checked in, but didn't connect with anyone, and nobody said they connected with them.
  • Didn't check in: kids who didn't check in and weren't picked by anyone, so we don't know how they got on. A kid who didn't check in but was picked by someone counts as connected.
  • How much the connections center on one kid: from 0 to 1. Close to 1 means most connections go through one kid. Close to 0 means they're spread out evenly. It needs at least 3 kids.
  • Connections per kid, on average: how many other kids each kid connected with, on average.
  • How close kids feel to the group: the average of the circle pictures, from 1 to 7. Higher means closer.
  • How much kids feel they belong: the average answer, from 1 to 5. Higher means they feel they belong more.
  • "answered" under a feeling number shows how many kids answered that question. Skipped answers are left out, so they don't pull the average down.
  • n/a means there isn't enough data to work that number out yet.

The picture

  • Each dot is a kid who was here, and each line joins two kids who connected.
  • Blue dot: connected with someone.
  • Orange dot: no connections.
  • Hollow grey dot: didn't check in.
  • Bigger dots have more connections.
