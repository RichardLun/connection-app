On this page

Each kind of question has its own section, and nothing is added up into one "connection score". Talking to lots of kids isn't the same as feeling you belong: a quiet kid may still feel they belong.

Who worked with or talked to whom

Two kids count as connected if either one said they worked with or talked to the other. Knowing each other before doesn't count. Only kids ticked as here are included.

  • Kids here: how many kids were ticked as here.
  • Checked in: how many of them filled in the check-in.
  • Connections: how many pairs of kids connected. If Ana and Ben both pick each other, that's still one connection.
  • Share of possible pairs who connected: from 0 to 1. 1 would mean every kid connected with every other kid. 0.20 means 1 in every 5 possible pairs connected.
  • Answered, but no reported connections: kids who answered the "who" questions (named someone or tapped Nobody), but didn't connect with anyone, and nobody picked them.
  • Not known: kids who skipped the "who" questions or didn't check in, and weren't picked by anyone, so we don't know how they got on. A kid who was picked by someone counts as connected.
  • How much the connections center on one kid: from 0 to 1. Close to 1 means most connections go through one kid. Close to 0 means they're spread out evenly. It needs at least 3 kids.
  • Connections per kid, on average: how many other kids each kid connected with, on average.

The picture

  • Each dot is a kid who was here, and the key under the picture says what each dot means. Bigger dots have more connections.
  • In a group with group-only reports, names are hidden and the dots are in a different order each session, so no kid can be picked out.

How kids felt about the group

  • Each belonging question gets its own average, with its scale, and how many kids answered and skipped it. Higher means closer or more included.
  • Skipped answers are left out, so they don't pull the average down.

Who to check on

Only shown if the group has leader support, and only on your screen. It's never in the report or the export.

Explain these results

Writes a short plain-English overview of the numbers, plus an idea for next time. Only group-level numbers are sent: no names or individual answers. Use it alongside what you saw, not instead of it.
