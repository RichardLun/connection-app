Connected

Welcome to Connected, a measurement tool for group connection!

What it is

Connected helps you measure connection within a group, and how it changes over multiple meetings.

How it works

  1. Create a group, then open it.
  2. Add people to your group!
  3. Start a session.
  4. Mark who's present.
  5. Have kids check in on their own phones by scanning a QR code, or one at a time on your device! Check-ins ask who kids worked with, talked to and knew before, and how connected they feel in the group.
  6. See the results based on the check-ins: how many connections occurred, how connected kids feel, and more. They're shown as both numbers and a network picture.
  7. See how individuals are interacting with the group, and how connected the group is as a whole. Warnings can show which kids might need help connecting.
  8. Once you've completed two or more sessions, you can compare them to see changes across time.

Happy connecting!
