Connected

Welcome to Connected, a measurement tool for group connection!

What it is

Connected helps you see how connected a group is, for a single activity or across the meetings of a group that meets regularly.

It keeps three things separate: who worked or talked with whom, how kids feel about the group, and what they learned. They're never added up into one score, because a quiet kid may still feel they belong.

How it works

  1. Create a group, choose how it will use Connected, then open it.
  2. Add people to your group!
  3. Start a session and pick the questions to ask. You can add an activity goal and a learning question too.
  4. Mark who's present.
  5. Have kids check in on their own phones by scanning a QR code, or one at a time on your device! Depending on the group, check-ins ask who kids worked with and talked to, how connected they feel to the group, and what they learned.
  6. See the results based on the check-ins: how many connections occurred, how connected kids feel, and more. They're shown as both numbers and a network picture.
  7. See how connected the group is as a whole. If you chose leader support for the group, warnings also show which kids might need help connecting.
  8. Open the group report to note what you'll try next time, and download or print it for staff.
  9. For a group that meets regularly, once you've completed two or more sessions, you can compare them to see changes across time.

Happy connecting!
