Groups

This screen gets built in Step 11 of docs/frontend-steps.md.

On this page

  1. Under Add a group, name your group, add the age range, and choose the program type.
  2. Then choose how the group will use Connected:
    • A group that meets regularly or A single activity. Only regular groups can compare sessions.
    • What kids are asked: their name and who they worked or talked with (this gives the network picture), their name and survey questions only, or survey questions with no names.
    • Group-only reports never name or flag a kid. Group reports, plus support for the leader also shows you which kids might need a check-in. Groups without names are always group-only.
  3. Click Save. Your new group's page opens straight away. You've created your first group! There you can add kids and start a session.
  4. When you come back here, your groups are listed with the newest at the top. Click a group to open it.
