Groups

This screen gets built in Step 11 of docs/frontend-steps.md.

On this page

  1. Under Add a group, name your group, add the age range, choose the program type, and click Save.
  2. Your new group's page opens straight away. You've created your first group! There you can add kids and start a session.
  3. When you come back here, your groups are listed with the newest at the top. Click a group to open it.
