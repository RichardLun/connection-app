On this page

A summary of this session for the group as a whole, to share with staff. It only shows what was actually collected.

What's in it

  • About this check-in: the date, activity, goal and what was asked.
  • How many kids attended, how many checked in, and for each question, how many answered and how many skipped.
  • Separate sections for who connected (if it was asked), how kids felt, activity feedback and the learning question. Learning answers are shown under the exact wording kids saw.
  • No names or flags, even if the group has leader support.

Staff notes

  • Use What will we try next? to record what you'll change, like showing the activity first. After trying it, fill in How did it go?
  • Write about the group, not individual kids: the notes are included in the export.

Saving and sharing

  • Download CSV gives the group-level numbers for a spreadsheet: no names, ids or individual answers.
  • Print or save as PDF makes a clean copy of the report.
  • Download GEXF saves the network for Gephi, one kind of connection per file.
